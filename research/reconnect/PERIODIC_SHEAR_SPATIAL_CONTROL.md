\[
\rho_0c_t=D\partial_a\{\rho_0[(1+\eta^2)c_a-\eta c_b]\}
+D\partial_b\{\rho_0[c_b-\eta c_a]\},
\quad \eta=0.25t/0.05\cos(2\pi b).
